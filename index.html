How the future MyCapital platform is separated

1. Customer experiencePersonal dashboard, customer portal, support and disclosures
2. Identity & accessVerified identity, authentication, roles and consent
3. Core platformAccounts, double-entry ledger, transaction orchestration and reporting
4. Partner boundaryBank / mobile-money / investment provider APIs and settlement reports
5. Safeguarded fundsFunds held by appropriately licensed partner institutions under approved arrangements

Non-negotiable separation

  • Customer assets are not company operating cash.
  • Each customer has a distinct account and ledger scope.
  • MyCapital's fees and company revenue are recorded separately.
  • Partner settlement balances are reconciled to platform records.

Build boundary

  • MyCapital manages instructions, records, controls and customer experience.
  • Licensed partners perform regulated custody, payment and investment functions.
  • Every external status must be confirmed by a trusted partner response or statement.
  • No balance is labelled available until its source and status are verified.

The future system uses separate identity, customer, account and permission records. A person can own more than one account; an account must always belong to one defined customer or legal entity.

Stage 2.1 · Integrated identity and access specification

These are target-system rules embedded in the existing Accounts & identity design. They are specifications, not live account records. No identity documents, passwords or customer data are collected or saved by this module.

1. Founder principal

  • One uniquely identified founder principal, separate from the company legal entity and every customer account.
  • Founder role grants platform governance access only within explicitly assigned scopes.
  • Privileged actions require strong MFA, step-up verification and an auditable reason.
  • Founder status never grants ownership of, custody over or unrestricted withdrawal rights to customer funds.

2. Customer identity

  • Support individual and legal-entity customer types with immutable customer IDs.
  • Separate customer profile, login user, financial account, consent and verification records.
  • One customer may have multiple users or accounts where legally and operationally appropriate.
  • Never use a phone number or email address as the permanent customer ID.

3. Authentication boundary

  • Production authentication must be server-side or delegated to a vetted identity provider.
  • Use modern password hashing, MFA, rate limits, breached-credential controls and secure session revocation.
  • Never store plaintext passwords, recovery secrets or session tokens in this prototype or browser localStorage.
  • Recovery must verify identity and notify the customer of sensitive changes.

4. KYC and verification

  • Lifecycle: not_started → submitted → under_review → verified / rejected / expired.
  • Restricted or enhanced review states must block only the actions defined by approved policy.
  • Store evidence in a separate encrypted, access-controlled evidence vault in production.
  • Keep provider references and review decisions in the platform; log every evidence access.

5. Roles and least privilege

  • Customer: own profile, consent and permitted accounts only.
  • Support: limited assistance metadata; no credential access or money movement.
  • Compliance: scoped KYC review and case access, with evidence access logged.
  • Security administrator: identity/security configuration, not unrestricted customer-funds authority.
  • Founder: governance scope; sensitive operational actions use maker-checker approval.

6. Account lifecycle

  • pending → active after required checks and approvals.
  • active → restricted / suspended only with a reason, actor and review path.
  • restricted / suspended → active only after authorised clearance.
  • active → closure_requested → closed after obligations and outstanding items are handled.
  • Closure never deletes ledger entries, audit history or records required by law.

7. Privacy and tenant isolation

  • Enforce customer/tenant isolation on the server for every read and write.
  • Apply data minimisation, purpose limitation, retention schedules and lawful deletion workflows.
  • Consent is purpose-specific, versioned, timestamped and revocable where applicable.
  • Staff access is time-bound, scoped and recorded; no shared staff accounts.

8. Append-only identity audit

  • Record actor, action, target, timestamp, reason, outcome and correlation ID.
  • Audit role grants/revocations, login/recovery events, KYC decisions and account-state changes.
  • Never place passwords, authentication secrets or full identity evidence in audit events.
  • Protect production audit storage against alteration and restrict who can query it.

Double-entry financial ledger

The production ledger is append-only. Every posted journal must balance: total debits equal total credits. Corrections use linked reversing journals and replacement journals; posted entries are never silently edited or deleted.

Ledger layerExample accountPurpose
Customer liabilityCustomer available / pending balanceWhat the platform owes or must account for on behalf of the customer, subject to legal structure
Partner asset / settlementSafeguarded partner settlement controlControl account reconciled against partner statements; not proof of custody by itself
Pending / suspenseUnmatched incoming paymentHolds uncertain items until confirmed and matched; never treated as spendable
Company revenueFee revenue receivable / earnedSeparates company fees from customer balances
Operational expenseProvider charge / operating expenseTracks company costs independently

Journal requirements

  • Unique journal and entry IDs
  • Currency and integer minor-unit amounts
  • Debit / credit direction
  • Source event and idempotency key
  • Effective time and recorded time
  • Actor, approval and audit references
  • Reversal linkage and reason

Posting controls

  • Reject unbalanced journals
  • Reject duplicate idempotency keys
  • Never overwrite posted entries
  • Restrict manual journals by role and approval
  • Reconcile ledger control accounts daily
  • Retain immutable audit events
This is the target production accounting model. It is not yet connected to or migrated from the Prototype 2.5 personal tracker ledger.

Partner-led deposit and withdrawal lifecycle

1. InitiatedCustomer requests an action; consent, limits and account status checked.
2. SubmittedMyCapital sends a uniquely identified instruction to an approved partner.
3. PendingAwait partner confirmation; do not show as settled or available.
4. ConfirmedVerify signed / authenticated partner status and transaction reference.
5. PostedPost balanced ledger journal exactly once using idempotency controls.
6. ReconciledMatch partner statement / settlement file; investigate breaks.

Deposit exceptions

  • Unknown reference → suspense and investigation
  • Duplicate callback → idempotent no-op
  • Reversed by partner → linked reversal journal
  • Amount mismatch → hold and reconcile

Withdrawal safeguards

  • Strong customer authentication
  • Available balance and limits check
  • Risk / fraud screening
  • Maker-checker approval where required
  • Partner final status before settlement claim

Security, privacy and operational controls

Identity & sessions

  • Multi-factor authentication for sensitive actions
  • Secure password hashing and session expiry
  • Device and session management
  • Step-up authentication for withdrawals and profile changes

Data protection

  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Field-level protection for highly sensitive data
  • Tenant isolation enforced server-side
  • Data minimization, retention and deletion policy

Staff controls

  • Role-based and attribute-based access
  • Least privilege and time-limited access
  • Dual approval for high-risk actions
  • Support staff cannot initiate fund movements

Resilience & monitoring

  • Encrypted backups and tested recovery
  • Immutable security and audit logs
  • Alerting for unusual access and transaction patterns
  • Incident response and customer notification process

Partners, legal readiness and launch gates

Partner responsibilities

  • Licensed bank / custodian safeguards funds under contract
  • Payment provider executes mobile-money and bank rails
  • Investment manager / provider executes approved investment activity
  • Independent audit and assurance providers test controls

MyCapital responsibilities

  • Customer interface, consent and instruction management
  • Accurate ledger, reconciliation and statements
  • Customer support, complaints and dispute tracking
  • Privacy, security, operational risk and disclosures

Uganda launch-readiness work

  • Obtain qualified legal advice on the exact business model and licensing perimeter
  • Confirm applicable Bank of Uganda, CMA and data-protection requirements with counsel and regulators
  • Agree safeguarding, settlement, dispute and audit responsibilities with licensed partners
  • Complete AML/CFT, KYC, consumer-protection and complaints frameworks

Production gates — none assumed complete

  • Legal / regulatory approval confirmed
  • Partner due diligence and contracts signed
  • Security architecture independently assessed
  • Penetration test and disaster recovery test passed
  • Reconciliation and dispute operations staffed
  • Controlled pilot approved before general release
